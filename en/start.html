Website check

Before I offer anything, the free check reviews your website automatically against up to 100 points, from security, legal pages and reachability to mobile, Google, speed and integrations such as forms, maps and booking. You see instantly what is missing and what to fix first. If you want to know more, book the in-depth check: I review by hand in eight areas, including texts, structure and user guidance, and you get a report with priorities, effort and a fixed price. €149 as a pilot price, fully credited against a new website.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a website cost?

The website in 7 days costs €390 as a pilot price for the first five clients, then €790. For comparison: freelancers usually charge €1,000 to €2,500 for a one-pager, agencies considerably more; the sources are on the packages page. You can add extra pages, copywriting or a second language as needed. Together with the inquiry automation you get a 10% bundle discount, and an in-depth check booked beforehand is fully credited. No VAT is charged because I’m a small business under § 19 UStG.

See packages and prices
Who owns the domain and website?

You do. Domain, hosting and accounts are in your name. If you want to switch later, you take everything with you.

What do I need to get started?

A completed questionnaire with your logo, photos, services, opening hours and the details for the legal notice. The 7 days start from then. To begin, the free website check is enough.
