Change time: what counts and what does not

Counts as a change

  • Changing or adding texts
  • Swapping images (you provide the files)
  • Opening hours, prices, contact details, team cards
  • Showing or hiding a notice banner or promotion
  • Adding links, downloads and PDFs
  • Adding or changing a form field
  • Changing the texts of the automatic emails (confirmation, review request)
  • Changing survey questions

Does not count (fixed price up front)

  • New page or new section with a new layout (fixed price, from €60)
  • New design, new colours, new logo (fixed price)
  • New feature: booking, gallery, login, shop, newsletter (fixed price)
  • Second language, copywriting, graphics (add-ons)
  • Reviewing or writing legal texts
  • Damage from third-party changes to hosting or domain (by effort)
  • Training beyond the handover (€60 per hour)

How it is counted

  • Time is recorded in 5-minute steps; every request counts at least 5 minutes
  • The allowance applies per calendar month; unused minutes expire at the end of the month
  • Beyond the allowance: €1 per minute (€60 per hour), announced beforehand
  • Send requests via the client portal (message “change request”) or by email; small changes are done within 2 working days (M) or 1 working day (L) after approval
  • The minutes are shown in the monthly report and in the client portal under “Maintenance”
  • Whether something counts as a change, I tell you beforehand; in doubt the list above decides

Examples: entering opening hours ≈ 5 min · swapping two photos ≈ 10 min · a new text section on an existing page ≈ 15 min · a promotion banner with text and date ≈ 15 min · updating a price list with 10 items ≈ 20 min · a new team member with photo and text ≈ 15 min
