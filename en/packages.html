How change time works

  • You send me your requests by email, WhatsApp or phone.
  • I change texts, prices, opening hours, images, offers or dates and add small new sections.
  • I record the time in 15-minute steps, the overview is in the monthly report.
  • Unused time carries over for one month.
  • For bigger jobs such as a new page or a new feature, I quote a fixed price up front.
  • Extra time costs €15 per started 15 minutes.

Updating your opening hours and swapping two photos, for example, takes about 15 minutes.
