Automation with n8n and AI

Recurring work such as sorting inquiries, moving data, reminding customers and creating reports runs by itself. You keep the overview and approve what matters yourself.

What can be automated

Wherever you carry data from A to B, write the same reply again and again or have to remember something, automation can help. I look at your process and build exactly the part that costs time.

  1. Inquiries and inbox

    Inquiries from forms, email or messengers land in one place, with a short summary and a notification on your phone.

  2. Quotes and documents

    Details from the inquiry flow into your template. You check, complete and send.

  3. Appointments and reminders

    Confirmations, reminders before the appointment and follow-ups after the job run automatically.

  4. Data between tools

    Spreadsheet, calendar, email and customer list stay in sync, without retyping.

  5. Reports and analysis

    Figures from forms, surveys or spreadsheets arrive regularly as a short report.

  6. AI assistant for text

    The AI summarises messages, translates, sorts and suggests replies. Nothing is sent until you approve it.

Frequently asked questions about automation

What is n8n?

n8n is a tool for connecting programs and services, for example email, spreadsheets, calendars and messengers. Every flow consists of individual steps that you can see and follow.

Do I need new software?

No. I connect the programs you already use. For you, usually the only change is that work disappears.

Does the AI decide anything on its own?

No. The AI only prepares things such as summaries, translations and reply drafts. You confirm commitments, prices and appointments yourself.

What does an automation cost?

That depends on the scope. After the conversation you get a fixed price. I tell you about possible running costs, for example for a server or an AI service, in advance. For inquiries from your website form there is the inquiry automation as a fixed package.

To the inquiry automation
Who is it worth it for?

For businesses where the same tasks come back every week, such as answering inquiries, confirming appointments, moving data and creating reports.

How does the AI know my prices and rules?

I gather with you what already exists, such as your price list, past quotes, text templates and fixed agreements. That becomes your business knowledge, and you can extend it at any time. If something is missing, the system asks instead of guessing.

How is this different from ChatGPT?

A general chatbot does not know your business and guesses prices. Here the AI works with your own documents, inside your tools and only as far as you approve.
