Privacy policy

The German version (Datenschutzerklärung) is legally binding.

1. Controller

Adrian Semlinger, Lou Digital, Lachenweg 34, 76139 Karlsruhe, Germany, email: info@lou-digital.de

2. Principles

This website sets no cookies, uses no tracking or analytics tools and loads no content from third-party servers. Fonts and scripts are hosted on this server. Only a short selection (your package selection) is kept in your browser’s session storage when you move between pages; it is deleted when you close the tab.

3. Hosting and server log files

The website is hosted by netcup GmbH, Daimlerstraße 25, 76185 Karlsruhe, Germany, on servers in Germany. A data processing agreement under Art. 28 GDPR is in place with netcup.

When you visit the website, the server automatically stores technical data: IP address, date and time, page requested, browser and operating system, and the referring page. This serves the secure and stable operation of the website (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR). Log files are deleted after 14 days at the latest unless they are needed longer to investigate a security incident.

4. Enquiry form and email

When you use the enquiry form, your details (topic, name, company if given, email address, phone number and website if given, message, your package selection if any) are sent encrypted to the web server and forwarded by email to my mailbox at netcup. You receive an automatic confirmation at the address you entered. To prevent abuse, a shortened, irreversible form of your IP address is kept for at most one hour to limit too many enquiries in a short time.

I process the details to answer your enquiry and, if applicable, prepare a quote (Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR). Only name and email address are required, plus the website address for a website check. I delete enquiries once they are settled and no statutory retention obligations apply; records of orders are kept for the periods required by tax and commercial law. The same applies if you email me directly.

5. Website check

For the free website check, I visit the public website you name and check it on five points. I store the result with your enquiry in order to send it to you (Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR).

6. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and to object to processing based on Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR. You can withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future. An email to info@lou-digital.de is sufficient.

You can lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information Baden-Württemberg, Lautenschlagerstraße 20, 70173 Stuttgart, Germany.
