Privacy policy

The German version (Datenschutzerklärung) is legally binding.

1. Controller

Adrian Semlinger, Lou Digital, Lachenweg 34, 76139 Karlsruhe, Germany, email: info@lou-digital.de

2. Principles

This website sets no cookies and loads no content from third-party servers. Fonts and scripts are hosted on this server. Only a short selection (your package selection) is kept in your browser’s session storage when you move between pages; it is deleted when you close the tab. To improve the website, I use my own audience measurement without cookies (section 6).

3. Hosting and server log files

The website is hosted by netcup GmbH, Daimlerstraße 25, 76185 Karlsruhe, Germany, on servers in Germany. A data processing agreement under Art. 28 GDPR is in place with netcup.

When you visit the website, the server automatically stores technical data: IP address, date and time, page requested, browser and operating system, and the referring page. This serves the secure and stable operation of the website (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR). Log files are deleted after 14 days at the latest unless they are needed longer to investigate a security incident.

4. Inquiry form and email

When you use the inquiry form, your details (topic, name, company if given, email address, phone number and website if given, message, your package selection if any) are sent encrypted to the web server and forwarded by email to my mailbox at netcup. You receive an automatic confirmation at the address you entered. To prevent abuse, a shortened, irreversible form of your IP address is kept for at most one hour to limit too many inquiries in a short time.

I process the details to answer your inquiry and, if applicable, prepare a quote (Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR). Only name and email address are required, plus the website address for a website check. I delete inquiries once they are settled and no statutory retention obligations apply; records of orders are kept for the periods required by tax and commercial law. The same applies if you email me directly.

If you request an appointment, I also process the preferred day, time slot and type of call in order to arrange it with you (Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR). You can send me suggestions via the feedback tab; an email address is optional there and only used for questions.

5. Website check

For the website check, my server visits the public website you name once and checks it on five points. With the instant check, the result is only displayed and not stored. If you request the check via the form, I store the result with your inquiry in order to send it to you (Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR).

6. Audience measurement without cookies

To understand which content is read and used and to improve the website, a script on this server records during your visit: the page viewed, buttons and links clicked with their approximate position, how far a page was read, forms submitted (without their content), the domain of the referring page, language, device type, operating system and browser. No cookies are set and nothing is stored in your browser. Your IP address is not stored; it is only briefly combined with the browser identifier and a random value that changes daily into a shortened hash so that visitors can be counted per day. This does not allow conclusions about you as a person. The data stays on the web space at netcup and is not shared. I delete raw data after 14 months.

The legal basis is my legitimate interest in a user-friendly and effective website (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR). If your browser sends “Do Not Track” or “Global Privacy Control”, no measurement takes place. You can object at any time; an email is sufficient.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and to object to processing based on Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR. You can withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future. An email to info@lou-digital.de is sufficient.

You can lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information Baden-Württemberg, Lautenschlagerstraße 20, 70173 Stuttgart, Germany.
