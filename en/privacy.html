Privacy policy

The German version (Datenschutzerklärung) is legally binding.

1. Controller

Adrian Semlinger, Lou Digital, Lachenweg 34, 76139 Karlsruhe, Germany, reachable by email at info@lou-digital.de

2. Principles

This website sets no cookies and loads no content from third-party servers. Fonts and scripts are hosted on this server. Only a short selection (your package selection) is kept in your browser’s session storage when you move between pages. It is deleted when you close the tab. Pages appear in the language of your browser (German or English). If you switch the language yourself, your browser remembers this choice in its local storage (localStorage) so the website appears in your chosen language on your next visit. This is required for this function you requested (Section 25 (2) no. 2 TDDDG) and can be deleted in your browser settings at any time. To improve the website, I use my own audience measurement without cookies (section 6).

3. Hosting and server log files

The website is hosted by netcup GmbH, Daimlerstraße 25, 76185 Karlsruhe, Germany, on servers in Germany. A data processing agreement under Art. 28 GDPR is in place with netcup.

When you visit the website, the server automatically stores technical data, namely IP address, date and time, page requested, browser and operating system, and the referring page. This serves the secure and stable operation of the website (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR). Log files are deleted after 14 days at the latest unless they are needed longer to investigate a security incident.

4. Inquiry form and email

When you use the inquiry form, your details (topic, name, company if given, email address, phone number and website if given, message, your package selection if any) are sent encrypted to the web server and forwarded by email to my mailbox at netcup. You receive an automatic confirmation at the address you entered. To prevent abuse, a shortened, irreversible form of your IP address is kept for at most one hour to limit too many inquiries in a short time.

I process the details to answer your inquiry and, if applicable, prepare a quote (Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR). Only name and email address are required, plus the website address for a website check. I delete inquiries once they are settled and no statutory retention obligations apply. Records of orders are kept for the periods required by tax and commercial law. The same applies if you email me directly.

If you request an appointment, I also process the preferred day, time slot and type of call in order to arrange it with you (Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR). You can send me suggestions via the feedback tab. An email address is optional there and only used for questions.

4a. Customer management and digital assistant

Inquiries and messages you send to Lou Digital via the form, by email or via a messenger are additionally stored in a customer management system on my own server (Oracle, data centre in Frankfurt, Germany). Only I have access, protected by password and a second factor.

So that inquiries are answered quickly, a digital assistant evaluates incoming messages. Voice messages are converted into text on my own server, and the text is sent to the AI service Claude by Anthropic PBC (USA), which summarises it, translates it and checks which details are missing. Anthropic processes the data on my behalf and, according to its own statements, does not use it for training. The transfer to the USA is based on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. Commitments, appointments and prices are always confirmed by me personally.

For new inquiries I receive an internal notification via the messenger Telegram (Telegram FZ-LLC, Dubai) with the name, a short summary and the text of the message. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR (inquiry and contract) and (f) GDPR (fast handling). If you do not want the assistant to evaluate your messages, just let me know. I will then handle your messages personally only. Inquiries without a contract are deleted after six months, customer data three years after the last contact, invoice data after eight years.

4b. Customer portal

Clients with a contract receive access to the customer portal at lou-digital.de/portal, where they see the status of their project, checks of their website (availability, response time, certificate), their maintenance plan, workflows, documents and messages. Stored are email address, name, company, phone, website, a password hash, optionally a key for the second factor, messages and a log of logins. The portal only sets a strictly necessary session cookie (ldp, until logout, at most 30 days) and a short-lived notice cookie; no consent is required for these (§ 25 (2) TDDDG). The help assistant in the portal answers questions automatically; the question and project details are sent to the AI service Claude (Anthropic PBC, USA, see section 4a). Legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR. Data is deleted three years after the contract ends; clients can download an export of their data in the portal at any time.

5. Website check

For the website check, my server visits the public website you name and checks it on 100 points. To do so it loads the homepage, the contact page, some subpages and linked pages of the same website, files such as robots.txt, security.txt and the sitemap, reads the certificate and queries public DNS records of the domain, for example for email. It only sees what any visitor can see and does not fill in any forms.

With the instant check, the result is only displayed in your browser and not stored. To prevent abuse, the server stores a non-reversible checksum of your IP address for at most one hour to limit the number of checks (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR).

Below the result you can request the detailed result sheet. Only when you submit this form are your details (name, email, optionally company and phone, your message) sent to me together with the checked address, the score and a list of the missing points. I store them with your inquiry in order to send you the result sheet and answer your questions about it (Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR). The retention periods from section 4 apply. To measure the loading time on a phone, I pass only the address of your website to the service Google PageSpeed Insights (Google Ireland Limited). I do not pass on your name or contact details.

6. Audience measurement without cookies

To understand which content is read and used and to improve the website, a script on this server records the page viewed during your visit, buttons and links clicked with their approximate position, how far a page was read, forms submitted (without their content), the domain of the referring page, language, device type, operating system and browser. No cookies are set and nothing is stored in your browser. Your IP address is not stored. It is only briefly combined with the browser identifier and a random value that changes daily into a shortened hash so that visitors can be counted per day. This does not allow conclusions about you as a person. The data stays on the web space at netcup and is not shared. I delete raw data after 14 months.

The legal basis is my legitimate interest in a user-friendly and effective website (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR). If your browser sends “Do Not Track” or “Global Privacy Control”, no measurement takes place. You can object at any time, and an email is sufficient.

6a. Profiles on Instagram and Facebook

Lou Digital has profiles on Instagram and Facebook, operated by Meta Platforms Ireland Limited, Merrion Road, Dublin 4, D04 X2K5, Ireland. This website does not embed any content from Meta and uses no plugins. The footer only contains plain links. Instagram or Facebook only opens when you click a link, and Meta’s privacy policy then applies.

When you visit my profiles, Meta processes data about you, even if you have no account there, for example for advertising and for statistics on use (“Insights”). Meta and I are joint controllers for these statistics (Art. 26 GDPR). Under an agreement, Meta takes primary responsibility for this and handles requests regarding your rights. I only receive aggregated figures, no data on individual people. Meta also transfers data to the USA and is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

If you write to me via Instagram or Facebook, I use your details to reply (Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR for inquiries about an order, otherwise (f)). The legal basis for the profiles themselves is my legitimate interest in staying in touch with prospects and customers (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR). More information is available in the privacy policies of Facebook and Instagram. You can exercise your rights with Meta and with me.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and to object to processing based on Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR. You can withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future. An email to info@lou-digital.de is sufficient.

You can lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information Baden-Württemberg, Lautenschlagerstraße 20, 70173 Stuttgart, Germany.
