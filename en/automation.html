Who it is for

For businesses that get inquiries through their website and do not want to lose any of them. Especially if you are often on the road and do not see inquiries at your computer right away.

Your customers get a confirmation immediately, you keep all inquiries in one place and see at a glance what is still open.

What you need

  • A website with a form, which I can also build for you
  • A spreadsheet that collects the inquiries
  • Your phone for the message
  • No extra program, no installation
  • Ready in 5 days

Automate more?

The inquiry automation is the entry point. With n8n and AI, further flows can be connected, such as AI summaries and reply suggestions, reminders before appointments or a monthly report.

Questions about the inquiry automation

What happens if I have no time right now?

Your customer immediately gets the automatic confirmation. The inquiry waits neatly in your spreadsheet until you get back to them.

Do I need a separate program?

No. All you need is your phone and a spreadsheet. The automation runs on tools that keep running after the handover.
