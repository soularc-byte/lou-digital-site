What the check typically finds

Much of this goes unnoticed day to day, but it costs visitors, trust or, in the worst case, a formal legal warning.

  • Address

    The address jumps around

    Anyone typing your address ends up on a third-party site-builder address via several detours. That confuses visitors and Google.

    your-company.com→http://www.your-company.com→your-company.builder.site

    ✕
  • Privacy

    Google loads without consent

    Fonts, maps or videos from Google load before visitors agree. In Germany this has already led to waves of legal warnings.

    • fonts.googleapis.combefore consent
    • maps.googleapis.combefore consent
    • youtube.com/embedbefore consent
    ✕
  • Mobile

    Content sticks out at the edge

    A table or image with a fixed width pushes the page sideways on a phone. Visitors have to swipe back and forth.

    ✕
  • Security

    Certificate expires soon

    Without automatic renewal, the browser will show a warning page instead of your website within days.

    Certificate for your-company.comexpires in 9 daysAutomatic renewal is off

    ✕

The ten critical points

If one of them is missing, you lose visitors, trust or risk a formal warning. Critical points count five times in the result.

  1. Secure connection with a valid certificateWithout a valid certificate the browser shows “Not secure” or blocks the site. Many visitors leave straight away.
  2. Old address redirects to the secure oneAnyone typing your address without https or following an old link otherwise lands on an insecure page. Google then counts two versions.
  3. Legal notice one click awayFor business websites in Germany the legal notice is mandatory. If it is missing or hidden, legal warnings are likely.
  4. Privacy policy reachableEvery website processes data, if only through the server. The GDPR therefore requires a privacy policy.
  5. No third-party services without consentThese services learn your visitors’ IP addresses. Without consent this is not allowed, and it has already led to waves of legal warnings.
  6. One clear address, with and without “www”Two separate versions confuse Google and visitors. If the site ends up on another domain, it looks dubious.
  7. Set up for phonesOver half of visitors use a phone. Without this setting a tiny desktop page appears.
  8. Meaningful page titleThe title decides whether your site is found and clicked on Google.
  9. Open to GoogleA blocked site does not appear on Google at all. This often happens when the block is forgotten after a relaunch.
  10. Contact directly from the homepageAnyone who cannot see right away how to reach you goes to the competition.

Security15 points
  1. CriticalSecure connection with a valid certificateWithout a valid certificate the browser shows “Not secure” or blocks the site. Many visitors leave straight away.
    How the check works
    The check opens your homepage with https:// and checks whether the browser accepts the certificate without a warning.
    What to do
    Activate a free Let’s Encrypt certificate at your host and switch on automatic renewal.

    I fix this as a one-off repair, usually in under an hour.

  2. CriticalOld address redirects to the secure oneAnyone typing your address without https or following an old link otherwise lands on an insecure page. Google then counts two versions.
    How the check works
    The check opens your address with http:// (without s) and checks whether the server permanently redirects to https://.
    What to do
    Set up a permanent redirect (code 301) from http to https, usually a switch at the host.

    I fix this as a one-off repair, usually in under an hour.

  3. ImportantCertificate is valid long enoughIf the certificate expires, every visitor sees a warning page instead of your website until someone renews it.
    How the check works
    The check reads the expiry date of your certificate and checks whether it is valid for at least 21 more days.
    What to do
    Check and switch on automatic renewal at your host.

    I take care of this in website maintenance from €29 per month.

  4. ImportantCertificate covers all addressesIf one variant is missing, some visitors see a security warning depending on how they type the address.
    How the check works
    The check tests whether the certificate is issued for your address with and without “www”.
    What to do
    Have the certificate issued for both variants.

    I fix this as a one-off repair, usually in under an hour.

  5. ImportantBrowsers remember the secure connectionWith it the browser always opens your site encrypted, even on public Wi-Fi. An attacker can then not quietly redirect the connection.
    How the check works
    The check reads your server’s response and looks for the “Strict-Transport-Security” (HSTS) instruction of at least 30 days.
    What to do
    Add one line to the server settings.

    I fix this as a one-off repair, usually in under an hour.

  6. ImportantAll images and files load securelyInsecurely loaded files break the padlock in the browser or are not shown at all.
    How the check works
    The check looks on the homepage for images, scripts and videos still embedded via http:// instead of https://.
    What to do
    Switch the addresses of the affected files to https://.

    I fix this as a one-off repair, usually in under an hour.

  7. ImportantSite cannot be embedded secretlyOtherwise a foreign site can overlay yours invisibly and trick visitors into clicks they did not intend.
    How the check works
    The check looks for the instruction that your site may not be embedded in other sites (X-Frame-Options or frame-ancestors).
    What to do
    Add one line to the server settings.

    I fix this as a one-off repair, usually in under an hour.

  8. RecommendedFiles are not misinterpretedIt stops the browser from running a harmless file as a program. That closes a well-known attack gap.
    How the check works
    The check looks in your server’s response for the “X-Content-Type-Options” instruction with the value “nosniff”.
    What to do
    Add one line to the server settings.

    I fix this as a one-off repair, usually in under an hour.

  9. RecommendedRules for where content may come fromIf foreign code ever gets onto the site, the browser blocks it. That is strong protection against data theft.
    How the check works
    The check looks for a “Content-Security-Policy”, a list of the addresses that scripts, images and fonts may be loaded from.
    What to do
    Set up a suitable policy and test it so nothing wanted gets blocked.

    I take care of this in website maintenance from €29 per month.

  10. RecommendedVisited addresses are not passed onWithout a rule, full addresses, for example with search or form data, can end up at other sites.
    How the check works
    The check looks for a “Referrer-Policy” that defines how much of the current address is passed to other sites.
    What to do
    Add one line to the server settings.

    I fix this as a one-off repair, usually in under an hour.

  11. RecommendedCamera, microphone and location lockedEmbedded third-party content can then not secretly request these features.
    How the check works
    The check looks for a “Permissions-Policy” that switches off unneeded features such as camera, microphone or location.
    What to do
    Add one line to the server settings.

    I fix this as a one-off repair, usually in under an hour.

  12. RecommendedNo software versions visibleVersion numbers show attackers which known flaws they can try.
    How the check works
    The check tests whether your server or system reveals version numbers such as “Apache 2.4” or “WordPress 6.1”.
    What to do
    Switch off the version display in server and system.

    I fix this as a one-off repair, usually in under an hour.

  13. RecommendedContact for security reportsAnyone who finds a flaw then knows whom to tell before someone exploits it.
    How the check works
    The check tests whether the file “/.well-known/security.txt” exists, stating where security flaws can be reported.
    What to do
    Create a short text file with an email address and expiry date.

    I take care of this in website maintenance from €29 per month.

  14. RecommendedCookies are protected against snoopingUnprotected cookies, for example for logins, are easier to steal.
    How the check works
    If your homepage sets cookies, the check tests whether they are only sent encrypted (“Secure”) and locked for scripts (“HttpOnly”).
    What to do
    Adjust the cookie settings in the system or on the server.

    I fix this as a one-off repair, usually in under an hour.

  15. RecommendedNo outdated system visibleOutdated systems are the most common way websites get hacked.
    How the check works
    If your site reveals a website builder or CMS with a version (e.g. WordPress), the check compares the version with the current release.
    What to do
    Update the system, plugins and themes and keep them maintained.

    I take care of this in website maintenance from €29 per month.

Frequently asked questions about the website check

What exactly does the check look at?

It checks the 100 points from nine areas listed in full above. These are security, legal and privacy, address and reachability, mobile and display, Google and visibility, speed, contact and trust, integrations and interfaces, and the email records of your domain. Only what any visitor can see from outside is checked.

What do Critical, Important and Recommended mean?

Critical are the ten points where you lose visitors straight away or risk a formal warning, such as a missing legal notice or an insecure connection. Important are 30 points that noticeably cost trust, visibility on Google or inquiries. Recommended are 60 points that turn a good website into a very good one. In the overall score, critical points count five times, important ones twice and recommended ones once.

What do you check under integrations and interfaces?

This covers everything that connects your website with other services, such as contact form and spam protection, online booking, Google map, WhatsApp or messenger, videos, social media profiles, Google profile and reviews, newsletter with confirmation, chat widgets and a shop with terms and cancellation policy. Whether third-party services load only after consent is especially important.

How do I know how a point is checked?

Every point has an info button. It explains in plain words how the check works, why the point matters and what to do. In the result, every missing point also says how I can solve it.

Is my address or the result stored?

Not with the instant check. The result only appears in your browser. To prevent abuse, the server keeps a non-reversible checksum of your IP address for at most one hour to limit the number of checks. Only when you request the result sheet below the result and submit the form do I receive your details together with the checked address, the score and the missing points.

Does the check replace legal advice?

No. It shows, for example, whether the legal notice and privacy policy are linked and whether third-party services load before consent. Only a lawyer can say with certainty whether the texts are correct.

Why do some points say “not applicable” or “could not be checked”?

Not every point applies to every website. If your site has no shop, for example, it needs no cancellation policy, so the point says “not applicable”. “Could not be checked” means a point could not be determined this time, for example because the server did not respond or a requirement is missing. The count is always out of 100, and neither lowers the overall score.

What does it cost to fix the points?

Many points are a server setting done in minutes, others take more work. I fix single points as a one-off repair, ongoing care is covered by website maintenance from €29 per month, and with a new website in 7 days all points are done from the start. I tell you the price beforehand.
