What I check

Encryption
Does your site run on HTTPS? Without the padlock, browsers warn your visitors.
Mobile
Is everything readable and usable on a phone? That’s where most visitors come from.
Legal notice
Is a legal notice present and reachable? It’s mandatory for businesses in Germany.
Privacy
Is there a privacy policy? Without one, you risk formal warnings.
Page title
Does the title say what you offer and where? It decides whether Google finds you.
