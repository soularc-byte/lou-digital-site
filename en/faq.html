Frequently asked questions

The key answers on prices, process and maintenance.

How much does a website cost?

The website in 7 days costs €390 as a pilot price for the first five clients, then €790. For comparison: freelancers usually charge €1,000 to €2,500 for a one-pager, agencies considerably more; the sources are on the packages page. You can add extra pages, copywriting or a second language as needed. Together with the inquiry automation you get a 10% bundle discount. No VAT is charged because I’m a small business under § 19 UStG.

See packages and prices
Who owns the domain and website?

You do. Domain, hosting and accounts are in your name. If you want to switch later, you take everything with you.

What do I need to get started?

A completed questionnaire with your logo, photos, services, opening hours and the details for the legal notice. The 7 days start from then. To begin, the free website check is enough.

To the website check
Do you write the legal notice and privacy policy?

I set up the structure and build the pages. The texts come from you or from a generator. For a binding review, it’s best to consult a lawyer.

What’s included in maintenance?

In every plan I check every 5 minutes that your website is reachable, back it up daily, keep it up to date and keep an eye on the SSL certificate and the domain. Once a month you get a short report. S costs €29 a month and keeps backups for 30 days; M with 30 minutes of changes, weekly updates, a form test and a reply within one working day €49; L with one hour of changes, 12 months of backups, a same-day reply and a quarterly website check €79. All plans can be cancelled monthly, and with annual payment two months are free. The full table is on the packages page.

Compare maintenance plans
What do 30 minutes or one hour of changes mean?

That’s the time I set aside for your requests every month. You send them by email, WhatsApp or phone, and I change texts, prices, opening hours, images, offers or dates or add small new sections. I record the time in 15-minute steps, the overview is in the monthly report, and unused time carries over for one month. For bigger jobs such as a new page, I quote a fixed price up front. Extra time costs €15 per started 15 minutes.

Build your package
How much do add-ons cost?

Every base package can be extended, for example with an additional page for €60, a second language for €190 or an additional channel for the inquiry automation for €90. Without maintenance you book changes individually at €15 per started 15 minutes; emergency help costs €95 per hour. All add-ons are listed with prices on the packages page, the builder adds up the total instantly, and with your inquiry you receive a non-binding price overview by email.

How quickly will you get back to me?

You get a confirmation by email right away and a personal reply from me, usually within 24 hours on working days. If I take longer, you get 10% off your first package.

Do you only work in Karlsruhe?

I’m based in Karlsruhe and happy to work with you in person. I also like to travel, so appointments outside Karlsruhe are no problem. I discuss it with you briefly up front and include travel and time in the price. Everything else runs by email and phone across Germany.
