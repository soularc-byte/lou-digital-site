Frequently asked questions

How much does a website cost?

The website in 7 days costs €390 as a pilot price for the first three clients, then €790 to €990. No VAT is charged because I’m a small business under § 19 UStG.

Who owns the domain and website?

You do. Domain, hosting and accounts are in your name. If you want to switch later, you take everything with you.

What do I need to get started?

A completed questionnaire with your logo, photos, services, opening hours and the details for the legal notice. The 7 days start from then.
